Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our plans, billing, and services.

What payment options do you support? +

We support payments in USD via Stripe/PayPal for international clients and INR via local bank transfer or UPI for clients based in Chennai.

Can I upgrade, downgrade, or cancel my plan? +

Yes! Our services are billed on a flexible month-to-month basis. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time with a 14-day notice before your next billing cycle.

Are there any hidden setup fees? +

No, there are no hidden setup fees. The monthly price listed is exactly what you pay for the services outlined in each tier.

What if I need custom deliverables not listed in the plans? +

We can easily customize any package to suit your specific requirements. Please reach out to our team via the contact form or WhatsApp to get a custom quote.

Is there a discount for annual billing? +

Yes, we offer a 20% discount on all plans if you choose yearly billing instead of monthly billing.
