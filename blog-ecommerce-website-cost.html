Ecommerce Website Cost in Chennai: A Detailed Breakdown

Date: July 30, 2026 • By: TIA E-commerce Team • 6 min read

Retail commerce in Chennai is changing rapidly. With the increase in digital transactions, setting up an online store is no longer just for big retail brands. Every local boutique, food brand, and manufacturer needs direct channels to sell to consumers.

If you're looking to build an online shop, this guide outlines the average ecommerce website cost in Chennai across three primary platforms: WooCommerce, Shopify, and Custom Headless builds.

1. WooCommerce (WordPress) Store

WooCommerce is a free plugin built on top of WordPress. It is highly customizable, and you own the database completely.

Average Build Cost: ₹25,000 to ₹45,000

Recurring Cost: Hosting (₹4,000 to ₹10,000/year) + plugins (₹5,000/year).

Ideal for: Small catalogs (under 200 products) and business owners who want complete control over code and configurations.

2. Shopify (SaaS Engine) Store

Shopify is a subscription service that hosts and runs your store for you. It features drag-and-drop systems, stable cloud hosting, and pre-built payment connections.

Average Build Cost: ₹25,000 to ₹50,000 (Development setup fees)

Recurring Cost: Shopify Monthly Plan (approx. ₹2,500/month) + Transaction fees + Premium App templates.

Ideal for: Rapid launch setups, drop-shippers, and companies who prefer a managed platform over self-hosting maintenance.

"While SaaS stores are easy to launch, recurring fees and app subscription costs can cut into your profit margin. Custom storefronts offer better long-term ROI."

3. Custom React & Headless E-commerce Store

For custom product builders, memberships, multi-vendor marketplaces, or high-performance brands, headless engines are the ultimate setup. The backend database (like Shopify or custom databases) is separate from the frontend user interface (built with React/Next.js).

Average Build Cost: ₹70,000 to ₹1,80,000+

Recurring Cost: Micro-hosting servers (₹5,000/year) + Database integrations.

Ideal for: Fast page speed, custom user accounts, complex product builders, and robust security protection.

Essential Integrations to Budget For

Building the site is step one. Ensure your developer integrates these necessary APIs:

  • Payment Gateway: Razorpay, Instamojo, or Paytm setup (usually free setup; 2% commission per transaction).
  • Logistics APIs: Shiprocket or Delhivery integration to automate shipping rates and track orders automatically.
  • SMS Gateway: Alert notifications for order confirmations (such as Twilio or MSG91).
